Ampule Support

Help with the Ampule app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Contact

Write to support@akars.me. Tell us the iPhone model, the iOS version and what you saw. Please do not send your log or any health details by e-mail unless we ask for them.

Common questions

My purchase does not show on another device

Open Me, then Ampule Plus, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that bought Plus. Plus works on all your devices through your Apple ID.

How do I cancel Ampule Plus?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Ampule Plus. Cancelling does not lock your log. Everything you entered stays readable and exportable.

How do I get my data out?

Me, then Export your data. You can export JSON or CSV, free, at any time. You can also bring a JSON or CSV file back in.

How do I delete my data?

Me, then Delete all data. This removes the log from your phone, and from iCloud and your other devices when iCloud sync is on. It cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

The reminder does not arrive

Check that notifications are on for Ampule in Settings, then Notifications, and that reminders are on in Me. Reminders say only "Dose day" and never name your medicine.

The Watch does not show my plan

Open Ampule on your iPhone once, so it can send the plan to the Watch.

Medical questions

Ampule records what you take. It does not choose a dose and it cannot answer medical questions. Ask your clinician or pharmacist. If you have severe symptoms, such as severe stomach pain that does not stop, repeated vomiting, or signs of an allergic reaction, get urgent medical help now. In Ampule, Urgent help is one tap from Today.
