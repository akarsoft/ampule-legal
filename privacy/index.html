Ampule Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Ampule is a log for GLP-1 doses, weight, protein, water and side effects. This policy says what happens to what you enter.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone: doses, medicine names, weights, intake, side effects, notes and your questions for your doctor. Ampule has no account, no server of its own and no advertising. We do not receive your data and we cannot read it.

What leaves your phone, only when you choose it

  • iCloud sync (Plus). If you turn it on, a copy of your log is stored in your own private iCloud, so your devices share one log. Only you can read it. Delete all data in Ampule removes it from iCloud and from your other devices.
  • Apple Health. If you turn it on in Me, Ampule can write weight, protein and water to Health, and read body mass from Health. If you turn on the Medications option, Ampule can read the medications you choose in the Health app and the doses you marked as taken for them, and add those doses to your log on your phone. You pick each medication in the system sheet. Ampule never writes a dose to Health. Each type is its own permission. Ampule never writes estimates, your medicine or your dose, and never uses Health data for marketing. What Ampule reads from Health stays on your phone: it is never sent to iCloud, not by sync and not by sharing.
  • Sharing. The appointment summary PDF and the data export are made on your phone. They leave it only when you share them.
  • Purchases. Ampule Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and billing. We see no payment details.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking. No analytics that identify you. No sale or use of your health data for marketing. No referral money from telehealth or pharmacies.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They say only "Dose day" and never name your medicine. Discreet mode, Face ID lock and a blurred app switcher are available in Me.

Your control

Everything you entered stays readable and can be exported as JSON or CSV at any time, free. Delete all data in Me removes it from your phone (and from iCloud when sync is on). Deleting the app removes the local copy.

Age

Ampule is for adults 18 and over.

Not medical advice

Ampule records what you take. It does not choose a dose, diagnose or replace your clinician. The estimated level is a model based on the manufacturer's prescribing information, not a measurement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is in the app.

Contact
