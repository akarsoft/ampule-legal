Ampule Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Ampule is a log for medicine doses, weight, protein, water, side effects and daily routines. This policy says what happens to what you enter.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone: doses, medicine names, weights, intake, side effects, routines, notes and your questions for your doctor. Ampule has no account, no server of its own and no advertising. We do not receive your data and we cannot read it.

What leaves your phone, only when you choose it

  • iCloud sync (Plus). If you turn it on, a copy of your log is stored in your own private iCloud, so your devices share one log. Only you can read it. Delete all data in Ampule removes it from iCloud and from your other devices.
  • People you look after (Plus). You can keep a separate log for another adult you care for. Each person has their own log on your phone. With iCloud sync on, their names and logs are stored in your own private iCloud, like yours. Nobody else can read them, and removing a person deletes their log from your phone.
  • Lab reports. With Plus you can choose a PDF or a photo of a lab report. Ampule reads it on your phone with the system's text recognition, lists the values it finds, and adds only the ones you tick. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the file is not kept.
  • Shared care (Plus). If you invite someone, they see your next dose and your last doses, read only, and only the extras you turn on (weight, side effects). It goes through your own iCloud as an Apple share; you can stop sharing at any time.
  • Write it. When you type a sentence to log a dose, it is read on your phone, with Apple’s on-device model where available. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is saved until you confirm.
  • Apple Health. If you turn it on in Me, Ampule can write weight, protein, water and the symptoms you mark (nausea, vomiting, constipation, diarrhea, heartburn, fatigue and headache, with the severity you chose) to Health. If you turn each on, it can also read body mass from your scale, and the protein, water and those symptoms that other apps save in Health, and show them beside what you enter; samples Ampule saved itself are never read back. In Keep your muscle, if you allow it, Ampule reads your strength workouts and lean body mass to show them beside your protein; they are read when the screen is open and are not stored. In Sleep, steps and glucose, if you allow it, Ampule reads your sleep, step count and blood glucose to show weekly averages beside your doses; they are read when the screen is open, are not stored, and Ampule does not judge them. If you turn on the Medications option, Ampule can read the medications you choose in the Health app and the doses you marked as taken for them, and add them to your log on your phone, each medication as its own medicine or added to one you choose, kept up to date while you use the app. You pick each medication in the system sheet. Ampule never writes a dose to Health. Each type is its own permission. Ampule never writes estimates, your medicine or your dose, and never uses Health data for marketing. In the United States, if your hospital shares lab results with Health, Ampule can list them and add the ones you tick to your lab list. Ampule only reads them, does not interpret them, and never writes them back. What Ampule reads from Health stays on your phone: it is never sent to iCloud, not by sync and not by sharing.
  • Sharing. The appointment summary PDF and the data export are made on your phone. They leave it only when you share them.
  • Purchases. Ampule Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and billing. We see no payment details.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking. No analytics that identify you. No sale or use of your health data for marketing. No referral money from telehealth or pharmacies.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They use neutral words such as “Dose day” or “How is your stomach today?” and never name your medicine. An answer you tap on a notification is saved on your phone like any other entry. Discreet mode, Face ID lock and a blurred app switcher are available in Me.

Your control

Everything you entered stays readable and can be exported as JSON or CSV at any time, free. Delete all data in Me removes it from your phone (and from iCloud when sync is on). Deleting the app removes the local copy.

Age

Ampule is for adults 18 and over.

Not medical advice

Ampule records what you take. It does not choose a dose, diagnose or replace your clinician. The estimated level is a model based on the manufacturer's prescribing information, not a measurement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is in the app.
